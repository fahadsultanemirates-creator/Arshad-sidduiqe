Copy Trading

Learn From Others. Stay in Control.

Copy trading lets eligible users follow and copy the trades or strategies of elite traders through Bitget's supported tools. You choose who to follow, how much to allocate, and when to stop.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Browse elite traders and bot strategies on futures, spot and bots.

  2. 2

    Evaluate

    Review history, risk, drawdown and style — not just headline returns.

  3. 3

    Follow

    Set your amount and risk controls, then copy the trader or bot.

  4. 4

    Monitor

    Track performance and adjust or stop copying at any time.

Futures copy trading

Copy elite futures traders. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.

Spot copy trading

Copy spot trades of elite traders on supported assets.

Bot copy trading

Copy automated strategies such as spot and futures grid bots.

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Copy trading is not passive or guaranteed income — copied trades carry the same market risk, and followers pay profit-sharing to the trader they copy in addition to trading fees.
