What is Bitget?

Bitget is a digital-asset platform offering products such as spot trading, futures, copy trading and trading bots, alongside the Bitget Wallet app and the Bitget Wallet Card. Available products depend on your jurisdiction.

What is copy trading?

Copy trading lets eligible users follow and automatically copy the trades or bot strategies of elite traders. You choose who to follow and how much to allocate. Copied trades carry the same market risk, profit-sharing and fees apply, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

What is the Bitget Wallet Card?

It is a crypto payment card issued through the Bitget Wallet app, usable with Visa and Mastercard merchants. Bitget currently advertises 2% base assetback, rising to 3% for new users or after a monthly spend threshold, on eligible purchases — subject to caps, conditions and Bitget's terms.

Does the card work in every country?

No. Card availability, eligibility and benefits vary by country and are subject to Bitget terms. Check eligibility in the Bitget Wallet app.

Are trading profits guaranteed?

No. Crypto and derivatives trading involve substantial risk and may result in loss. Nothing on this website is financial advice, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
